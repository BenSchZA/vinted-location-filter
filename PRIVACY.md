# Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Vinted Location Filter is a browser extension that shows which country each
Vinted search result is sold from and lets you filter results by country.

## What it collects

Nothing. The extension collects no personal information, sends no data to the
developer, and uses no analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

## What it stores, and where

Two things are stored locally in your own browser, using the browser's
extension storage:

- Your filter settings: the countries you selected, whether non-matching
  results are hidden or faded, and whether cities are shown.
- A cache of the country and city already read for a seller, so the same seller
  does not have to be requested from Vinted again. Vinted limits how often that
  can be requested, and the cache is what keeps the extension within that limit.

Neither leaves your device. Neither is readable by the developer.

## Network requests

The extension makes requests only to the Vinted site you already have open, and
only to Vinted's own interfaces: the catalogue search used to list results, and,
when you turn on the city option, the seller profile that holds the country and
city. These are the same requests the Vinted page itself makes.

No request is sent to the developer or to any third party.

## Sharing

No data is sold, transferred, or shared with anyone, because none is collected.

## Deleting your data

The cached seller list can be cleared at any time with "Clear cached sellers" in
the extension popup. Removing the extension from your browser deletes the
settings and the cache along with it.

## Permissions

The extension requests access to Vinted's country domains only, each listed
individually in its manifest, and the browser's storage permission for the two
items described above. It requests nothing else.

## Source
