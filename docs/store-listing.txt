Vinted mixes sellers from other countries into your search results with no way
to tell them apart. This extension tags every result with the seller's country
and filters by it.

Pick one or more countries. The rest are hidden, or faded if you prefer to keep
seeing them. The country list comes from the search you are viewing, with a
count beside each, so it works on any Vinted site. Controls sit in a bar above
the results and in the toolbar popup, and both change the same setting. Results
load as you scroll rather than page by page.

The country comes from the currency the seller priced in, which is already in
Vinted's search response, so filtering costs no extra requests.

Limits: about eighteen markets price in euros, and there the currency cannot
separate one country from another, so the country needs a slower per-seller
lookup. City is off by default for the same reason, and sellers who leave it
blank show the country only. A seller whose country cannot be read is tagged
amber.

No account, no analytics. It talks only to the Vinted site you have open and
stores settings in your browser.
